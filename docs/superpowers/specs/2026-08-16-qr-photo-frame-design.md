# Wedding QR Photo Frame Design

## Goal

Create a print-ready 4×6-inch portrait card that presents the existing Arshi and Amar seating-chart QR code in a small wedding photo frame.

## Layout

- Ivory background with a thin gold border and restrained botanical flourishes.
- Header: `ARSHI & AMAR`.
- Supporting title: `Find Your Table`.
- Existing QR centered in the middle with its encoded modules unchanged.
- Call to action: `Scan to find your table`.
- Small closing line: `We’re so glad you’re here`.

## Deliverables

- Print-ready 4×6 portrait PNG at 1200×1800 pixels (300 DPI equivalent).
- Matching SVG for crisp printing and future resizing.

## Constraints and verification

- Preserve the exact existing QR payload and QR artwork.
- Keep sufficient clear space around the QR for reliable scanning.
- Confirm the output is visually balanced at 4×6 proportions.
- Publish the finished assets under `assets/` and verify they return HTTP 200 through GitHub Pages.
