# Themed Wedding QR Design

## Goal

Create a phone-sharing-friendly QR code for `https://riddhishpandya.github.io/arshiandamar/` that visually matches the wedding site.

## Design

- Use an ivory background aligned with the site’s paper palette.
- Use deep forest-green QR modules aligned with the site’s ink color.
- Add a restrained gold border and floral accents outside the QR quiet zone.
- Keep the QR modules unobstructed: no logo, text, or decoration over the encoded area.
- Preserve a generous white/ivory quiet zone for reliable phone scanning.

## Deliverables

- A themed PNG suitable for messaging and social sharing.
- A themed SVG suitable for crisp resizing and print fallback.

## Verification

- The encoded URL remains exactly the live GitHub Pages URL.
- Both assets are committed and published through GitHub Pages.
- The published PNG and SVG return HTTP 200.
- The visual QR remains high contrast and scannable.
